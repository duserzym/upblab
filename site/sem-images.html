UPb Lab / Review: SEM images

SEM images in Review

Review’s SEM images panel shows the published atlas in a read-only GrainAtlas viewer.

Read the published atlas

Enable SEM images in Review’s scope bar. The panel uses the bundled GrainAtlas build in a read-only viewer presentation. Spots are coloured by sample using Review’s colours, and the mount map inset in the corner shows their position on the mount. Use Fit to fit the view and the area menu to choose an area. Maximise the panel for a closer look, then restore it to see the plots beside it.

Connect images to results

Selecting a linked spot in the SEM images panel selects its result in Review. Selecting a result in Review focuses the corresponding image spot. See SEM images beside the plots.

See sample groups on the mount

With SEM images on and no single spot selected, the mount map inset outlines where each shown sample’s spots sit on the mount in that sample’s Review colour. The inset’s header counts the samples. This group view appears after opening a scope, toggling Group unregistered by spot name, or using sample chips, sample rows, All, Samples or Standards. Picking a single spot returns the inset to that spot.

Edit and publish in GrainAtlas

Review’s Edit in grainatlas opens the associated project in standalone GrainAtlas on desktop, or its hosted build in a new tab on the web. Home’s Upload image set, opened or dropped image folders, .grainatlas folders and .gaproj files also open GrainAtlas there.

Use GrainAtlas to stitch images, segment grains and place spots. Its Publish button publishes the atlas to a mount on UPbDB. Editing happens in GrainAtlas; UPb Lab shows the published atlas. See the GrainAtlas repository.
