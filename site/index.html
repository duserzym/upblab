UPb Lab / Overview

One window for the lab workflow.

UPb Lab connects SEM images, laser sessions and stored U-Pb results for the University of Minnesota geochronology lab.

Install and sign inRead the Review guide

From an image to its result

UPb Lab connects GrainAtlas, the lab database server, UPbDB, and UPbRPy Studio with one sign-in. A spot in an image links to its stored result. GrainAtlas and UPbRPy Studio open as separate desktop applications; on the web, GrainAtlas’s hosted build opens in a new tab.

Tabs are Home, Sessions, Review and Reduce (Ctrl/Cmd 1–4), plus People & access for accounts allowed to manage people.

  1. ImagesStitch, segment and place spots in standalone GrainAtlas.
  2. Session uploadStore the laser session and spot links in UPbDB.
  3. ReductionReduce in UPbRPy Studio and submit the results.
  4. ReviewInspect stored results beside SEM images in UPb Lab.
The shell does no data reduction.

UPb Lab links the modules and displays results already stored in UPbDB. Editing images and reductions happens in GrainAtlas and UPbRPy Studio.

The connected apps

  • GrainAtlas: SEM grain-mount images, stitching, segmentation, spot placement and atlas publishing.
  • The lab database server, UPbDB: sessions, spots and stored reductions.
  • UPbRPy: UPbRPy Studio and reduction.
  • upb-standards: reference material values used in reduction.
