UPb Lab / Home

Start with your files.

Home brings opening files, uploads, sessions, mounts and atlases into one board.

Open or upload

  • Open folder and Open files route files by their contents. You can also drop them on the app window.
  • Upload LA-ICP-MS session sends a Rich CSV, per-spot export folder, zip, or a Rich CSV with its per-spot folder to Sessions. Confirm the ingest there.
  • Upload image set opens standalone GrainAtlas on desktop, or its hosted build in a new tab on the web. Opened or dropped image folders, .grainatlas folders and .gaproj files go there too. Publish the atlas from GrainAtlas to link it to a mount; Home’s mount cards show its publish status.

Return to work

Use the Sessions and Mounts cards to open a session, reduce it in UPbRPy Studio, or review stored results. Search and sort the grids to find what you need. Module status is shown at the top of Home.

The session upload panel checks for an uploaded session and a completed reduction every 5 s for up to 10 minutes while it remains open. Dismissing it stops the watch. Review becomes available once a complete reduction run is stored.

Follow an app link

Paste a upbdb://, grainatlas:// or upbr:// link into Home’s Links box and choose Open. The shell routes it to the matching module or application.
