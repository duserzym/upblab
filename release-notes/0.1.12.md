- Home: Laser log hands one Chromium .lase file to UPbDB, which uploads it and opens the tie dialog for the day's sessions.
- Session cards show a tied laser log and whether it has laser times (needs a UPbDB with laser logs; older servers show nothing).
- TIMS batches: a guided rail (Batch & capsule set, Grains, Spike, Chemistry, Review & complete) with Next incomplete; each capsule shows whether its grain and spike are recorded.
- TIMS spike weighing is required per capsule: pinned spike version, weighed mass (direct or bottle before/after), balance readability, analyst and UTC time. The server's tracer amounts appear after saving; stale edits show both entries to compare. Spike edit history opens on demand.
- Statuses from spiked to measured need a recorded spike. Complete batch and Reopen batch follow the server's readiness (needs a UPbDB with batch completion; older servers say so).
- TIMS capsules show a loose grain (picked for TIMS only, never mounted; needs a UPbDB with loose grains) by its label with "Loose grain" instead of a mount, and a fixed placeholder instead of a GrainAtlas image. Replace grain and Refine in GrainAtlas are disabled for loose grains.
